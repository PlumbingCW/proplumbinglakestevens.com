Burst pipe: do these things first

A burst pipe is an emergency at any hour. Our 24/7 line answers every call. These steps reduce damage while you wait.

  1. Shut off the main water valve. It is usually near the water heater, in the crawlspace or at the meter. Turn it fully clockwise.
  2. Cut power to wet areas. Turn off the breakers for rooms with water. Do not touch outlets or switches with wet hands.
  3. Open faucets. Open the lowest faucet to drain the lines and relieve pressure.
  4. Move belongings. Lift rugs, electronics and valuables out of the water path.
  5. Call us. Describe where the water is and what you see.

What causes pipes to burst

Freezing is the best-known cause. Water expands as it freezes and splits pipe walls, often in crawlspaces, exterior walls and unheated garages. Corrosion, high water pressure and age are the other common reasons. Many bursts happen when thawing releases pressure at a split.

Cold snaps do reach Western Washington and Snohomish County. Insulating exposed pipes, disconnecting hoses and knowing where the main shutoff sits protect your home before the next cold spell.

Prevent the next burst

A few steps cut the risk before winter.

  • Insulate exposed pipes in crawlspaces, garages and exterior walls.
  • Disconnect garden hoses and shut off outdoor spigots before a freeze.
  • Let faucets drip on the coldest nights where pipes run along outside walls.
  • Learn the main shutoff location and test that it turns.
  • Keep the heat on at 55 degrees or above if you travel.

Code and standards for the repair

Replacing a burst section of pipe follows the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), as adopted in Washington with state amendments in WAC 51-56, which governs approved materials and joints. Document the damage with photos before cleanup, since your insurer may ask for them.

Questions we hear

Do you handle burst pipes at night or on weekends in Lake Stevens?

Yes. Our line is open 24/7. Shut off the main water valve first, then call us.

How do I find my main shutoff?

Look near where the water line enters the house, near the water heater, in a crawlspace or at the meter box in the yard.

Can a frozen pipe be thawed safely?

Sometimes. If a pipe has not burst, warm the area slowly. Never use an open flame. Call us if you are unsure.

Will insurance cover a burst pipe?

Many policies cover sudden water damage. Check with your insurer and document everything.
