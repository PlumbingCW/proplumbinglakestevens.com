After-hours plumbing emergencies for businesses

When a tenant space floods or a restaurant drain backs up, every hour costs money. Our 24/7 line takes commercial calls, and we shut off water, clear blockages and make temporary repairs first, then discuss a lasting fix.

Before we arrive, find the building's main shutoff, move inventory off the floor and keep staff away from standing water near electrical equipment. If you do not know where the shutoff is, tell us the building type and we will talk you through finding it.

Grease traps, backflow testing and storm drains

Grease traps and interceptors keep fats out of the sewer, and they need regular cleaning. A full trap stops working, backs up into the kitchen and can bring fines. We pump and clean traps and report on their condition.

Backflow assemblies protect the public water supply from contamination and are tested on a schedule set by the water provider. Washington certifies backflow specialty plumbers through the Department of Labor and Industries. Ask your water provider when your assembly is due.

Storm drains and catch basins on commercial lots clog with leaves, silt and trash. Cleaning them before the rainy season prevents ponding in parking lots and loading areas.

Commercial leak detection

Large buildings can lose water for weeks without anyone noticing. A rising water bill, a wet ceiling tile or a persistent smell is the first sign. We use listening equipment and thermal imaging to find leaks in slabs, walls and ceilings, and give you a report you can show a landlord or insurer.

Some commercial leak calls end without a repair booking because the building owner, not the tenant, controls the work. We still help tenants document the problem so the right person can act.

Code and standards for business plumbing

Commercial work follows the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) as adopted in Washington with state amendments, WAC 51-56, which includes rules for grease interceptors, backflow prevention and fixture counts. State rules require backflow assembly testing to be done by a certified tester, so ask for proof of certification and keep the test report for your records.
