What is verified about Rhodora Heights

Rhodora Heights was named in coverage of the Southeast Interlocal Annexation, which the City of Lake Stevens completed in August 2021 without a public vote. The annexation covered about 500 acres plus the lake and an estimated 1,000 properties, west of 123rd Avenue SE and north of 20th Street SE. Rhodora Heights Road and nearby streets use ZIP code 98258.

What this means for you: your address is now inside the city, which can change who handles permits and city services. Ask the City of Lake Stevens what applies to your property.

What we do not know about Rhodora Heights

We have not verified the age of the homes, the pipe materials or which properties use a public sewer rather than a septic system. We will not guess at these, because the answer differs from lot to lot. The lake shore and the hillside may also differ in how water drains, and we have no data on that.

Do not assume your home has a particular pipe or a particular problem because of where it sits. A visit and a sewer camera inspection when needed give you facts about your own lines.

Who to ask about water and sewer

Snohomish County PUD supplies water in the Lake Stevens area, and its water line is 425-397-3000. The Lake Stevens Sewer District serves the city for sewer, but not every property is necessarily connected. Ask the district whether your address is on its system.

You can check a plumber's certification or a contractor's license on the Washington Department of Labor and Industries Verify page. The City of Lake Stevens can tell you whether a repair needs a permit.
