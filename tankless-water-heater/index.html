Tankless Water Heater Service in Lake Stevens, WA

Tankless heaters deliver continuous hot water, but they need the right gas line, a clean heat exchanger and a yearly flush. We install, repair and maintain gas and electric tankless units.

What tankless service covers

Tankless water heaters heat water only when a tap opens, so there is no tank to rust and no standby heat loss. In exchange, they have more electronics, tighter gas and venting needs and a heat exchanger that scale can coat. Service for these units splits into installation, repair and routine flushing.

We install gas and electric models, repair burner, sensor and flow problems, and flush and descale units on a regular schedule. Because a tankless failure often shows up as an error code, tell us the model and code when you call.

Flush and descale: the maintenance most owners skip

Descaling dissolves mineral buildup inside the heat exchanger. We connect a pump and a descaling solution to the service valves, circulate it through the unit and flush it clean. Many manufacturers recommend doing this on a schedule, and some warranties ask for proof.

Local water is not very hard, so buildup is slower here than in hard-water cities, but it is not absent. Snohomish County PUD lists average total hardness of 73 ppm for its Lake Stevens system, with a range of 9 to 89. Ask us for a flush schedule that fits your model, or check the manual.

Common tankless symptoms and what they point to

  • Error code on the panel: most codes cover ignition, flow, venting or sensor faults and tell us where to start.
  • Cold water between hot bursts: a flow rate too low to trigger the burner, or a mixing issue with fixtures.
  • Water too hot or too cold: a failed temperature sensor or thermistor.
  • Reduced flow: a scaled heat exchanger or a clogged inlet filter.
  • Unit shuts off mid-shower: a blocked vent, a gas supply that is too small, or a safety limit tripping.
Local note

Gas lines and venting in Lake Stevens homes

Tankless gas units use far more gas at peak than a tank heater. If the line serving your home was sized for a tank and a furnace, a tankless install may require a larger line. We check capacity before quoting so there are no surprises.

Venting runs through a wall or roof with specific clearances. Washington's state plumbing code adopts the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) with state amendments, and gas and venting work is inspected. Confirm permit requirements with the City of Lake Stevens building department.

Gas and exhaust safety: what to do right away

  • Smell gas? Leave the building, do not touch switches, and call 911 and your gas utility from outside.
  • Carbon monoxide alarm sounding? Get everyone outside into fresh air and call 911.
  • Soot, scorch marks or a vent that has come apart? Turn the unit off and call a plumber before using it again.
  • Error code on the display? Write it down and call before resetting it repeatedly.

Code and standards for tankless units

Tankless installs follow the manufacturer's installation manual and the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) as adopted in Washington under WAC 51-56. The manual lists the gas input, vent material, vent length limits and clearances for your exact model, and the plumber works to those numbers. Treat any model-specific figure in an online article as a starting point, and confirm it against the manual.

Questions we hear

How often should a tankless water heater be flushed?

Follow the manufacturer's schedule, which is often once a year. With local hardness in a modest range, some units go longer, but a yearly check is cheap protection.

Why does my tankless heater give me a cold blast between hot water?

Low flow can keep the burner from firing, and some fixtures mix cold water back in. We test flow at each tap to find the cause.

Can you install a tankless heater in place of my tank?

Often yes. We check gas line size, venting and electrical capacity first, since these decide whether the swap is simple or needs upgrades.

Do you repair tankless water heaters?

Yes. Brand and model matter, so give us the error code when you call and we will plan the parts.

Is an emergency tankless repair available at night?

Our line answers 24/7. If there is no hot water, we diagnose by phone first and schedule the repair, with a price quoted before work starts.
