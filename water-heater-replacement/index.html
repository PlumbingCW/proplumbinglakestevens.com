A failed water heater: what to do before we arrive

A tank that has rusted through can release up to 40 or 50 gallons across a utility room floor in a few hours. If you see water around the base, shut the cold supply valve above the heater and turn off the breaker or gas control. Then call our 24/7 line.

Replacement is the right call when the leak comes from the tank body itself, not a fitting. We confirm that at the door, then walk you through sizes and fuel options so you can decide the same day. A clear price comes before any work starts.

How a same-day replacement works

  1. Confirm the failureWe check whether the leak is the tank or a fitting that can be repaired.
  2. Choose the unitMatch capacity, fuel and venting to the old setup, or discuss an upgrade.
  3. Swap and connectDrain, disconnect, set the new tank, reconnect supply, venting and relief valve.
  4. Test and walk throughWe check for leaks, set the temperature and show you the shutoffs.

Choosing a size and fuel type

Tank size should follow how many people shower in the morning, not square footage. A 40-gallon tank is common for two or three people. Larger families often move to 50 or 75 gallons, or to a tankless unit that heats on demand.

Like-for-like replacement is fastest because the gas line, vent, drain and electrical stay where they are. Switching fuel or moving the heater adds permit and labor steps. We will say which route fits your house instead of defaulting to the most expensive one.

For a first look at options, see our tankless water heater page. If you are replacing a tank with a different type of unit, our water heater installation page covers the installation side.

What this means for Lake Stevens homeowners

Snohomish County PUD reports total hardness for its Lake Stevens water system between 9 and 89 ppm, averaging 73. That is a modest range, so a new tank here is less likely to scale up as fast as in very hard-water areas. A yearly drain of a few gallons and an anode rod check still protect the investment.

Code itemsExpansion tank, relief valve discharge, seismic strapping and a drain pan where required.

Standard homeowner policies usually cover sudden water damage to flooring but not the heater itself. Keep your photos of the failed tank if you plan to file a claim.

Code and standards behind a replacement

A replacement heater is installed according to the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), as adopted in Washington with state amendments under WAC 51-56, and the manufacturer's instructions. Those cover the relief valve and its discharge pipe, venting for gas units and a safe drain path. Ask the City of Lake Stevens building department whether your replacement needs a permit and an inspection.

Before the plumber arrives, clear the path

A few minutes of prep shortens the visit.

  • Move boxes and stored items at least 3 feet from the heater.
  • Clear a path from the front door and from any garage or crawlspace access.
  • Note the model and size from the label on the old tank.
  • Photograph the leak and the surrounding wet area for your records.

Questions we hear

Do you handle water heater replacement emergencies at night or on weekends in Lake Stevens?

Yes. Call the 24/7 line, and if the tank is leaking, shut off the cold water and the power or gas first. We will talk through options and schedule the replacement, quoting the price before work begins.

How long does a water heater replacement take?

A like-for-like swap in an accessible spot is usually a half day. Changes to venting, gas piping or location take longer, and we say so before we start.

Do you remove the old water heater?

Yes. We drain, disconnect and haul away the failed unit as part of the visit.

Is a tankless heater a better replacement than a tank?

It depends on your gas line, your hot water use and your budget. Tankless units save space and deliver continuous hot water, but they can need gas line or electrical upgrades.

Do I need a permit to replace a water heater?

Permit rules vary, so ask the City of Lake Stevens building department what applies to your replacement. We will tell you what the install involves so you can ask the right question.
