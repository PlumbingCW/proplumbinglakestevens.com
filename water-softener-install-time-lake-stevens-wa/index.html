How Long Does Water Softener Installation Take in Lake Stevens?

A straightforward softener install usually takes a few hours. A job that needs new drain lines, a bypass loop or electrical work can take a day or more.

Call (360) 371-6092

By the Lake Stevens Pro Plumbing team / Published November 13, 2026

Typical install time

A standard softener install, with a bypass valve, a nearby drain and an outlet, often takes about two to four hours. The plumber shuts off water, cuts into the supply line, installs the bypass and connections, sets the drain line and brine tank and programs the control head.

The time grows if the plumber has to run a drain line a long way, add an outlet, replace corroded pipe near the connection or move the unit to a different spot. Water is off during the install, so plan for a window without it.

How the install usually goes

  1. Check the siteConfirm the space, drain, outlet and supply line.
  2. Shut off and cut inClose the main, drain the line and install the bypass and unit connections.
  3. Set the drain and brine tankRun the drain line with an air gap and fill the tank with salt.
  4. Start up and programRestore water, flush the resin, set hardness and test.

DrainFloor drain next to the unitNew drain line over a long distance

Prep before the plumber arrives

  • Clear the install area and a path to it.
  • Locate the main shutoff and show the plumber.
  • Plan for water to be off for a few hours.
  • Buy salt or ask the plumber to supply it.
  • Test your water hardness so the unit is set correctly.
Local note

Lake Stevens water and install notes

Snohomish County PUD lists total hardness between 9 and 89 ppm for the Lake Stevens system, averaging 73 ppm, roughly 4.3 grains per gallon. The control head should be set from a test at your tap rather than a guess. A softener set too high wastes salt and water.

Brine discharge must go to an approved drain. Ask the Lake Stevens Sewer District about its rules, and ask the City of Lake Stevens building department if a permit applies. Washington's state plumbing code, WAC 51-56, adopts the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) with state amendments.

Lake Stevens grew from 6,361 residents in 2000 to 28,069 in 2010. In many newer homes, the garage is where the water heater and main shutoff live, which makes it a natural spot for a softener.

Salt, settings and keeping it running

A softener works by swapping hardness minerals for sodium or potassium, and it needs salt or a potassium substitute to do that. Add salt to the brine tank so the level stays above the water line, and keep the salt dry and free of bridges, the hard crust that sometimes forms and leaves an empty gap below. Break up a bridge gently with a broom handle.

Set the control head from a hardness test and your household size. A control set too high regenerates more often than needed, wasting salt and water. Many heads also let you pick a regeneration time, and overnight is the usual choice so water is soft when the household wakes.

Plan a yearly check. A plumber can clean the injector and screen, test the output hardness, check the bypass valve and confirm the drain line is clear. That small visit helps the unit last and keeps your water consistent.

After the install

Expect cloudy or air-filled water at first, so run a faucet until it clears. Check the connections for drips over the next day. Add salt as the manufacturer directs and check the brine tank every month or two. Sediment and age affect the system over time, so schedule a yearly check.

Questions we hear

How long does a softener install take?

A simple install often takes two to four hours. Added drain, pipe or electrical work can extend it.

Will my water be off during the install?

Yes, for part of the visit. Plan to be without water for a few hours.

Do I need a drain for a softener?

Yes. The system discharges water during regeneration, so it needs an approved drain with an air gap.

Is my Lake Stevens water hard enough to need a softener?

Snohomish County PUD lists hardness from 9 to 89 ppm, averaging 73. Test your tap first.

Do I need a permit?

Ask the City of Lake Stevens building department. Rules vary by project.
